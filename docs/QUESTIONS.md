
# HomeSure – 10 Project Questions

## 1. Project Registration
Is the apartment project registered under Tamil Nadu RERA?

## 2. Registration Number
What is the valid RERA registration number of the project?

## 3. Project Completion
What is the original expected completion date?

## 4. Project Extensions
Has the builder received any extension for project completion?

## 5. Advance Payment
Has the builder collected more than 10% of the apartment cost before signing a registered agreement for sale?

## 6. Carpet Area
Does the builder clearly mention the carpet area instead of using only super built-up area?

## 7. Agreement Clauses
Does the builder agreement contain any unfair or one-sided clauses?

## 8. Delay Compensation
What compensation or interest is applicable if the builder delays possession under RERA?

## 9. Complaints and Orders
Are there any complaints, disputes, or regulatory orders related to the project?

## 10. Project Health
Based on registration, completion, complaints, and regulatory orders, what is the overall project status?
